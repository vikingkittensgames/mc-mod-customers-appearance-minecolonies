# Customers MineColonies Appearance

Customers MineColonies Appearance is an optional NeoForge add-on for
[Customers](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/customers). It makes
Customers and Suppliers match the citizen texture style of the MineColonies
colony around their spawner.

[Learn more about MineColonies](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/minecolonies).

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.230 or newer in the 21.1 line
- Customers 2.1.0 or newer
- Architectury API 13.0.8 or newer in the 13.0 line
- MineColonies 1.1.1368 for Minecraft 1.21.1 and its normal dependencies

## Use the appearance

Install Customers, Architectury API, MineColonies and its dependencies, and
this add-on. Place a Customer Spawner or Supplier Spawner inside or near a
colony, open the spawner's appearance settings, and enable
`MineColonies - Local Colony`.

The containing colony is preferred. Otherwise, the nearest colony can be used
when it is within the configured distance. Spawned villagers keep their chosen
style after leaving the colony or reloading the world; later colony style
changes affect newly spawned villagers.
